Who sells to us

South Korea stayed in the top three every year. China jumped to 3rd in 2024 as Chinese beauty brands expand across South-East Asia. Japan slipped the other way, from 4th to 7th.

And who we sell to

Malaysia sells too: US$173 million in 2024, to 187 countries. Most of it stays close to home. Japan, a beauty giant itself, is our 3rd biggest buyer at US$13 million.

57%of our exports went to South-East Asian neighbours.

How to read it

Darker brick-red squares mean more products advertise it. Hyaluronic acid leads; oil-free claims are rare (4%). Eye creams lean hardest on hyaluronic acid: 22% advertise it.

Who is hardest to please

Whatever their skin type, reviewers give a typical product about 4.3★. But oily skin hands out top marks less often. Combination skin, common in humid weather, rates products almost exactly like normal skin.

4.33★typical rating from oily skin; the other skin types give 4.35–4.37★.

What to look forThe right-hand tails: the olive shape thins out above 4.5★.

Oily versus dry skin

Humid weather leaves many Malaysians with oily or combination skin. Most products please oily skin and dry skin alike, but some, especially sunscreens, split them by up to 1.5 stars.

72%of products get similar ratings from oily and dry skin.

What to look forThe biggest split: a moisturiser sold for "oil and pore control", rated 1.5★ lower by oily skin.

Need a sunscreen for oily skin? Each dot is one product, ranked by its oily-skin rating. The brand tells you more than the label: Shiseido sits near the top.

Three things to remember at the beauty counter

1

A higher price doesn't buy a better rating

Skincare under US$25 averages 4.17★; over US$250, 4.24★. Check reviews and ingredients before paying more.

2

Read reviews from people with your skin type

Most products suit oily and dry skin alike, but some sunscreens split them by up to 1.5 stars. Labels like "Best for Oily" barely help.

3

The label advertises; the ingredient list tells

Only 12% of skincare is fragrance-free and 4% oil-free. Check the full ingredient list, not just the box front.

These tips come from US Sephora products and reviews, so treat them as a guide rather than advice for your own skin.
